The public App Store version is **1.0.1**. FieldPad **1.1 is in validation**. No launch date is promised.

The current workflow centers on customer records, service jobs, equipment history, estimates, invoices and refrigerant records. Optional FieldPad Cloud, QuickBooks Online and Stripe connections are prerelease work. They are not advertised as working features of the current App Store version.

Before connected features are offered for general use, we need to finish provider staging, company isolation, offline and purchase restoration, upgrade and physical-device testing. QuickBooks Online scope will be stated explicitly; it does not mean QuickBooks Desktop or complete accounting automation.

**Pricing:** current U.S. public App Store list prices are $29.99/month and $249.99/year. Apple’s purchase sheet shows available plans, local prices and renewal terms. A planned weekly offer is not yet advertised as available.

**Refrigerant calculations:** current federal leak-repair thresholds for covered appliances are 10% comfort cooling, 20% commercial refrigeration and 30% industrial process refrigeration. Part 84 excludes residential/light-commercial AC and heat pumps from its leak-repair rule. Review applicability and calculations before relying on an older app result. FieldPad does not certify compliance.

Contact [FieldPad support](mailto:fieldpad@fieldpadpro.com) for release questions. Pilot participation is arranged individually after acceptance; this page does not enroll you or grant access.
