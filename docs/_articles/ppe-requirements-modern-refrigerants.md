## Select protection for the actual work

Refrigerant service can involve chemical exposure, cold liquid contact, pressurized equipment and other task-specific hazards. Identify the refrigerant and equipment, obtain the current safety data sheet, and review the manufacturer instructions before choosing protective equipment.

Where applicable, [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.132](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.132) requires a workplace hazard assessment, selection of suitable and properly fitting PPE, communication of the selection, and a written certification of the assessment. The appropriate eye, face, hand, foot and body protection depends on that assessment. A single list of gloves, clothing or shoes is not a universal requirement for every refrigerant and task.

## Respiratory protection needs a qualified program

Do not assume a half-face respirator with an organic-vapor cartridge makes an exposure safe because it is brief. An air-purifying respirator does not supply oxygen. An unknown or oxygen-deficient atmosphere requires evaluation under the specific respiratory-protection and entry requirements; do not enter or attempt a rescue based on this article or an app checklist.

[OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.134) requires appropriate hazard evaluation and respirator selection. Where respirators are required, the program includes qualified administration, medical evaluation, training and applicable fit testing. Contaminant, concentration, oxygen availability and equipment limitations matter. Use the site's qualified safety program and emergency procedures rather than choosing a cartridge from a generic refrigerant category.

## A2L prompts are records, not a complete procedure

An A2L classification does not determine every necessary precaution. EPA's [residential/light-commercial AC and heat-pump substitute listings](https://www.epa.gov/snap/substitutes-residential-and-light-commercial-air-conditioning-and-heat-pumps) include end-use conditions. Follow those conditions, the equipment instructions, safety data and applicable codes.

FieldPad's six safety prompts are documentation aids. Record the checks actually performed and retain the supporting assessment or service information. Completed checkboxes do not certify safe work or legal compliance. This article does not provide a refrigerant-handling, confined-space entry or rescue procedure.
