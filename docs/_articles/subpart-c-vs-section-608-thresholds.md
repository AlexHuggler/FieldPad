## Evaluate both frameworks independently

[40 CFR 82.157](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-82/subpart-F/section-82.157) generally covers qualifying ozone-depleting refrigerant appliances with at least 50 pounds of full charge. [40 CFR 84.106](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-84/subpart-C/section-84.106) applies from January 1, 2026 to covered appliances with at least 15 pounds of an HFC or substitute for an HFC with GWP greater than 53. Refrigerant identity alone does not determine the answer.

**Residential and light-commercial air-conditioning and heat pumps are excluded from Part 84's leak-repair requirements.** This does not remove separate certification, recovery or venting duties. Confirm the appliance's regulatory subsector instead of relying on a generic AC label.

| Covered category | Part 82 | Part 84 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comfort cooling | **10%** | **10%** |
| Commercial refrigeration | **20%** | **20%** |
| Industrial process refrigeration | **30%** | **30%** |

The earlier comparison of 15% versus 10% on this site was wrong. Crossing means **exceeding** the threshold, not merely equaling it. Part 84 also assigns 10% to covered other appliances, including transport categories under the current rule; a proposed exclusion is not an enacted change.

Part 84 has a special first-addition rule beginning in 2026, subject to its stated exceptions. Verification timing, recordkeeping and inspections must be evaluated against the relevant paragraphs.
