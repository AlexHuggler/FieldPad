Compare the work you actually do

Try the same service call in each app you are considering. Check the vendor’s current plan and purchase terms; prices and capabilities change.

  1. Find an existing customer and the equipment’s service history.
  2. Schedule a job, prepare an estimate and turn it into an invoice.
  3. Collect a signature and share a clear PDF with the customer.
  4. Disconnect from the network, restart the app and check what remains available.
  5. Restore an export on a clean device before relying on it as a backup.
  6. If you need accounting, verify the exact QuickBooks edition, supported records, taxes, payment handling and conflict behavior.

Where FieldPad stands

FieldPad focuses on customers, jobs, invoices, equipment history and refrigerant records for an owner-operator. The public App Store version is 1.0.1. Optional FieldPad Cloud, Stripe and QuickBooks Online connections are being validated for 1.1 and are not generally available.

Current U.S. public App Store list prices are $29.99/month and $249.99/year, with a free tier. Check Apple’s purchase sheet for your localized price and available plans.
